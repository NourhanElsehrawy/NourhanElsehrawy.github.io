Chapter 1

The Foundation: Learning Through Building

2017 - 2021 | Helwan University

My journey began in the lecture halls of Computer science department, where I was studying Computer Science. But the real learning happened outside the classroom - in the projects I built, the datasets I explored, and the problems I tried to solve.

During my final year, I chose to tackle something meaningful: predicting harmful drug interactions. This wasn't just an academic exercise - it was a real problem that could impact lives. I spent months working with the DrugBank dataset, experimenting with Random Forest, SVM, and neural networks.

The breakthrough came when I realized that the best model wasn't just about accuracy - it was about understanding the problem deeply. My MLP model achieved the highest precision and recall, but more importantly, I learned that data science is about asking the right questions, not just applying algorithms.

Key Learning: The best models come from understanding the problem domain, not just the algorithms.

Chapter 2

First Steps: From Intern to Impact

2021 | Master Linux → Chefaa Egypt

Fresh out of university, I joined Master Linux as a Data Annotation & Machine Learning Intern. This was my first taste of the real world - working with production NLP and computer vision models, learning how data quality shapes model performance, and understanding the importance of proper annotation workflows.

But the real turning point came when I joined Chefaa Egypt as a Data Scientist in November 2021. Here, I learned that data science isn't just about building models - it's about solving business problems.

My first major challenge: the company needed better insights, but manual reporting was eating up 70% of the team's time. I saw an opportunity to build something transformative - a Business Intelligence Hub that would automate workflows and provide real-time analytics.

I spent months building a web-based platform using Flask, SQL, and Power BI. The result? Over 500k EGP in revenue generated, making it the company's highest-earning analytics product. But more than the revenue, I learned that the best solutions come from understanding what users actually need, not what you think they need.

Impact: Automated 70% of manual reporting workload, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions faster.

Chapter 3

Scaling Impact: Production ML at Scale

2023 - Present | Jumia Group

In November 2023, I joined Jumia Group, Africa's leading e-commerce platform. This was a new level - millions of users, terabytes of data, and the challenge of building ML systems that scale.

I started in the Internal Audit team, but my passion for building production ML systems led me to transition to the Core Data Science team. Here, I work on recommender systems, fraud detection, and integrating LLMs into internal tools.

One of my proudest moments came when I improved recommendation accuracy by 15% through an A/B test comparing collaborative filtering strategies. But the real win wasn't just the metric - it was learning to collaborate with product teams, validate uplift through CTR analysis, and deploy models that users actually interact with.

Another breakthrough: I reduced product miscategorization errors by 20% by applying modern embedding techniques (Word2Vec, BERT-style encodings) and unsupervised clustering. This project taught me that sometimes the best solutions come from combining traditional ML with modern NLP techniques.

Currently, I'm exploring the frontier of GenAI - integrating LLMs into our analytics platforms to make data insights more accessible. It's exciting to be at the intersection of traditional ML and the AI revolution.

Current Focus: Building scalable ML systems and integrating LLMs to democratize data insights across the organization.
